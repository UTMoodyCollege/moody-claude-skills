Built by students, for everyone

Signal is produced by Moody students across the School of Journalism and Media, Radio-Television-Film and Advertising and Public Relations. Every session is captioned, the main stage is livestreamed, and evening screenings are open to the Austin community.

“We wanted a summit where students aren’t just in the audience — they’re on the stage, behind the cameras and in the control room.”

— Signal 2026 student producer (fictional)

Who should attend

  • Students curious about media careers
  • Faculty and researchers
  • Alumni and industry partners
  • Austin community members
